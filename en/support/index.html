Entering scores

Dartpop works alongside a real dartboard: you enter each dart by hand and the app does the math. During a game you can switch between the Touch dartboard and the Quick keypad at any time.

  • Touch dartboard. Tap the segment you hit, or drag your finger: a magnifier shows the segment before you let go. Around the board is a miss.
  • Quick keypad. Tap a number. For a double or a treble, select Double or Treble first; in ’01 you can also swipe up on the key for a double and down for a treble. The 25 and Bull 50 keys complete the keypad.
  • Miss. A dart off the board is entered with the Miss button.
  • Confirm. A turn is up to three darts; confirm it to move on to the next player. In ’01, with Automatic next player (Settings › At your pace), the turn is confirmed after a short delay once the third dart is in.
  • Correct. The undo button (the back arrow) removes the last entry. In ’01 it can also return to the previous turn, and tapping a dart you already entered lets you re-enter it and the later darts.

The rules of each game are explained in Settings › At your pace › Understand the rules.

The games

In the Play tab, choose a game, then the players: 2 to 8 (at least 3 for Killer).

  • 301 · 501. Start from 301, 501, 701, 901 or 1001. Straight in or double in, straight out or double out. Individual, pairs (2 vs 2) or teams (4 vs 4) with a shared score. One leg, or first to 2, 3 or 5 wins.
  • Cricket. Close 15 to 20 and the bull.
  • Around the Clock. Hit the numbers 1 to 20 in order, then the bull if that option is on.
  • Killer. Each player has a number and lives; the last player standing wins.
  • Shanghai. Each round aims at its own number, over 7 or 20 rounds. A single, a double and a treble in the same turn win at once.
  • Halve-It. Hit the target of each round; if all three darts miss it, your total is halved.

The Practice tab offers solo drills.

The free trial

Three full matches are free. A match includes all its legs, and only completed matches count. The number of free matches remaining is shown in Settings › Purchases.

After that, one permanent purchase lets you start new matches without limit. There is no subscription. The price is shown on the App Store, in your currency, before you buy.

Without buying, your results remain available, and solo practice, history, statistics, backups, sharing and the second screen stay free.

Restoring your purchase

  1. Use the same Apple Account as for the purchase.
  2. Open Settings › Purchases.
  3. Tap Restore purchases.

The app shows “Purchases restored” once access is back, or “No purchases to restore” if it finds none. Internet access may be required: if you read “Service unavailable. Try again later.”, check your connection and try again.

Moving to another device

  1. On the old device, open Settings › Backup and restore, then tap Export backup. Save the file to Files or iCloud Drive, or share it with your new device.
  2. On the new device, open the same screen, tap Import backup and pick the file.
  3. Restore your purchase separately with the same Apple Account: Settings › Purchases › Restore purchases.

The backup holds your saved friends, the current match and the results. Import replaces the active match and settings; existing results are kept. A recovery copy is created first: Recover the version before import takes you back.

The free-match counter is not reset by importing a backup.

Sounds and haptics

Both are set in Settings › At your pace and are on by default.

  • Sounds. Small sounds when a turn is confirmed, on a win and after a purchase. They are silent when the device is in silent mode and do not interrupt your music.
  • Haptic feedback. Vibrations when entering darts and ending the turn.

No sound? Check the device’s silent mode, then the Sounds switch.

Optional data sharing

Dartpop asks you once: “Help improve Dartpop?”. Sharing is optional and stays off unless you answer “Share”.

If you agree, the app sends usage statistics and crash diagnostics, with a random identifier. Never player names, scores or your backups.

You can change your mind at any time with the Settings › About › Help improve Dartpop switch. All the details are on the Privacy page.

Reporting a problem

Email us with:

  • your device model and iOS or iPadOS version;
  • your Dartpop version, shown in Settings › About › Version;
  • the game involved and the steps that lead to the problem.

Never send passwords or bank information.
