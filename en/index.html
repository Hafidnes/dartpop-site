Frequently asked questions

You
ask.

Do I need an electronic dartboard?

No. Dartpop works alongside a real, traditional dartboard. You enter each dart by hand on the touch dartboard or the quick keypad; the app does the math.

How many players?

2 to 8 players. In ’01 you can also play in pairs or teams of four with a shared score. Practice is solo.

What happens after the three free matches?

Your results stay available. One purchase unlocks unlimited new matches, with no subscription. Solo drills, history, statistics and backups remain available without buying.

Do I need internet or an account?

No. Games are played offline, with no account. The purchase goes through the App Store; you can restore it from the app’s Settings.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Your names, scores and games stay on your device. Sharing anonymous usage statistics is optional and off by default. All the details are on the Privacy page.

Which devices and languages?

iPhone and iPad, in portrait or landscape. In English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese.
